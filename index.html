Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Perfume Auto Poster ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information from general users. If you connect a Facebook Page or Telegram Channel to our application, we may receive access tokens and basic page/channel information necessary to publish content on your behalf. We do not store your personal passwords.

2. How We Use Information

We use the permissions granted through the Facebook and Telegram APIs strictly to publish posts, schedule content, and read basic insights as authorized by you. We use AI services to generate text based on the prompts you provide.

3. Data Retention

We do not sell your data to third parties. Any temporary data used for scheduling posts is deleted after the post is published or the schedule is canceled. Access tokens are stored securely and are only used to maintain the connection to your social media accounts.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application uses the following third-party services:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook): To publish and schedule posts to your Facebook Page.
  • Telegram: To send messages to your Telegram Channel.
  • AI Providers: To generate text content based on your instructions.

Your use of these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Deletion

You can request the deletion of your data at any time by removing our app from your Facebook Page settings or by contacting us directly. Once requested, we will delete your access tokens and any stored scheduling data from our servers.
